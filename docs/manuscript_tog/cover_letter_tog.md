To the Editor-in-Chief, Magy Seif El-Nasr, IEEE Transactions on Games

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I submit the manuscript "A Frame-Only Census of Game Mechanics Built on ARC-AGI-3 Failed Preregistered Transfer Tests on MiniGrid/BabyAI and Procgen" as a full paper, in the journal's category of negative and null results papers. It is not a candidate for a special issue.

Before any confirmatory run, I registered the claim that a fixed, frame-only description of game mechanics, the assumption census, built and frozen on eight ARC-AGI-3 games, would report the mechanics of unseen games from two other benchmarks as their source code states them, and would predict on which of those games assumption-driven frame-only agents complete the first level. I ran the census and three agents on 161 MiniGrid/BabyAI environments under two action conditions and on 14 Procgen games, 10,500 runs in all, and applied the registered tests and outcome table without change. The result is a refutation, by the registered clause "V1 fails on any suite": the census failed the validity test on both benchmarks, and its score passed the prediction test for no agent. The paper reports this as registered, with every deviation from the protocol, and keeps an exploratory diagnosis of why the instrument did not transfer apart from the registered results.
